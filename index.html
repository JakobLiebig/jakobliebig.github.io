Part 1

From noise to images

\( p(x) \) as a landscape

Height = how likely a pixel combination is a real image.

Forward diffusion

100 small Gaussian steps: every image walks downhill while the peaks melt into one \( \mathcal{N}(0, I) \).

Walking back up

Seen from above: how do we get from this noisy point back to its image?

Idea: a network predicts the noise added in this step, we subtract it, repeat.

Here is another zigzag from the same image. It lands on the same point \( x_t \).

But its last step was different. Best guess for this step's noise: \( \approx 0 \).

So predict the total noise: \( x_t = \sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}\,x_0 + \sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}\,\varepsilon \), learn \( \varepsilon_\theta(x_t, t) \).

\( x_{t-1} = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt{\alpha_t}}\big(x_t - \tfrac{\beta_t}{\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}}\,\varepsilon_\theta(x_t, t)\big) + \sigma_t z \)

DDPM

1000 steps = 1000 network calls.

Ho et al. 2020

Two motions

Each step moves every sample in two ways.

Deterministic: a pull toward 0.

Random: a small random kick.

Brownian motion

Random kicks + deterministic drift.

Brownian motion: continuous time

Forward diffusion is a stochastic differential equation (SDE)\( dx = \underbrace{f(x, t)\,dt}_{\text{drift}} + \underbrace{g(t)\,dW}_{\text{diffusion}} \)

Sampling is integration.

Brownian motion: it runs backwards

The density follows Fokker–Planck\( \partial_t p_t = -\nabla\cdot(f\,p_t) + \tfrac12 g^2\,\Delta p_t \)

Reversed in time (Anderson 1982)\( dx = \big[f - g^2\,\nabla_x \log p_t\big]\,dt + g\,d\bar W \)

Only unknown: the score. The model can learn it directly.

What is the score?

The uphill direction\( s(x) = \nabla_x \log p(x) \)

For one noised image\( \nabla_{x_t} \log q(x_t \mid x_0) = -\dfrac{\varepsilon}{\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}} \)

So noise prediction = score\( s_\theta(x_t, t) = -\dfrac{\varepsilon_\theta(x_t, t)}{\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}} \)

Brownian motion: a deterministic twin

Same \( p_t \), no noise\( dx = \big[f - \tfrac12 g^2\,\nabla_x \log p_t\big]\,dt \)Song et al. 2021, Score-Based Generative Modeling through Stochastic Differential Equations

Fewer steps: 25 instead of 100.

\( dx = \Big[f + \tfrac12 g^2\,{\color{#f2994a} \dfrac{\varepsilon_\theta(x, t)}{\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}}}\Big]\,dt \)

The path is long

Sampling walks the reverse SDE: 100 small steps, every step is one network call.

Without the noise (DDIM): one smooth path, but curved. 25 steps.

Flow matching: go straight from the noise to the image. A few steps are enough.

Training: an image \( x_0 \), noise \( \varepsilon \), a random \( t \). Interpolate \( x_t = (1-t)\,x_0 + t\,\varepsilon \) and learn the vector field along the line: \( v_\theta(x_t, t) \approx \varepsilon - x_0 \).

Three ways from noise to data

Two trained networks, same architecture: ε for DDPM and DDIM, velocity for flow matching. MLP, 4 layers × 256, SiLU, sinusoidal time embedding (≈ 207k parameters). Adam, lr \( 10^{-3} \) with cosine decay, batch 2048, 30k steps on fresh samples (≈ 61M samples, no epochs), ≈ 6 min each on a CPU.

\( x_t \)

\( x_T \)
\( q(x_t \mid x_{t-1}) \)
\( q(x_t \mid x_0) \)
\( p_\theta(x_{t-1} \mid x_t) \)

\( q(x_t \mid x_{t-1}) = \mathcal{N}\big(x_t;\ \sqrt{1-\beta_t}\,x_{t-1},\ \beta_t I\big) \)

\( q(x_t \mid x_0) = \mathcal{N}\big(x_t;\ \sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}\,x_0,\ (1-\bar\alpha_t) I\big), \quad \bar\alpha_t = \textstyle\prod_{s \le t} (1-\beta_s) \)

Backup: DDPM

The true reverse step

Forward step
\( q(x_t \mid x_{t-1}) = \mathcal{N}\big(\sqrt{\alpha_t}\,x_{t-1},\ \beta_t I\big), \quad \alpha_t = 1-\beta_t,\ \ \bar\alpha_t = \textstyle\prod_{s \le t} \alpha_s \)
Jump from \( x_0 \)
\( q(x_t \mid x_0) = \mathcal{N}\big(\sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}\,x_0,\ (1-\bar\alpha_t)\,I\big) \)
Reverse, if \( x_0 \) is known (Bayes)
\( q(x_{t-1} \mid x_t, x_0) = \mathcal{N}\big(\tilde\mu_t(x_t, x_0),\ \tilde\beta_t I\big) \)
Mean
\( \displaystyle \tilde\mu_t = \frac{\sqrt{\bar\alpha_{t-1}}\,\beta_t}{1-\bar\alpha_t}\,x_0 + \frac{\sqrt{\alpha_t}\,(1-\bar\alpha_{t-1})}{1-\bar\alpha_t}\,x_t \)
Variance
\( \displaystyle \tilde\beta_t = \frac{1-\bar\alpha_{t-1}}{1-\bar\alpha_t}\,\beta_t \)

At sampling time \( x_0 \) is unknown: the network has to stand in for it.

Backup: DDPM

The training objective

Model
\( p_\theta(x_{0:T}) = p(x_T) \textstyle\prod_{t=1}^T p_\theta(x_{t-1} \mid x_t), \quad p_\theta(x_{t-1} \mid x_t) = \mathcal{N}\big(\mu_\theta(x_t, t),\ \sigma_t^2 I\big) \)
Variational bound
\( -\log p_\theta(x_0) \le \mathbb{E}_q\big[\, L_T + \textstyle\sum_{t=2}^T L_{t-1} + L_0 \,\big] \)
One term per step
\( L_{t-1} = D_{\mathrm{KL}}\big(q(x_{t-1} \mid x_t, x_0)\ \|\ p_\theta(x_{t-1} \mid x_t)\big) \)
Two Gaussians, fixed variance
\( \displaystyle L_{t-1} = \frac{1}{2\sigma_t^2}\, \mathbb{E}_q \big\lVert \tilde\mu_t(x_t, x_0) - \mu_\theta(x_t, t) \big\rVert^2 + C \)
No parameters
\( L_T = D_{\mathrm{KL}}\big(q(x_T \mid x_0)\ \|\ \mathcal{N}(0, I)\big) \approx 0 \)
Backup: DDPM

Predict the noise

Write \( x_0 \) via \( \varepsilon \)
\( x_t = \sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}\,x_0 + \sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}\,\varepsilon \ \Rightarrow\ \displaystyle \tilde\mu_t = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\alpha_t}}\Big(x_t - \frac{\beta_t}{\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}}\,\varepsilon\Big) \)
Same form for the model
\( \displaystyle \mu_\theta(x_t, t) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\alpha_t}}\Big(x_t - \frac{\beta_t}{\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}}\,\varepsilon_\theta(x_t, t)\Big) \)
Loss per step
\( \displaystyle L_{t-1} = \frac{\beta_t^2}{2\sigma_t^2\,\alpha_t\,(1-\bar\alpha_t)}\ \mathbb{E}\big\lVert \varepsilon - \varepsilon_\theta(x_t, t) \big\rVert^2 + C \)
Ho et al.: drop the weight
\( L_{\text{simple}} = \mathbb{E}_{t,\,x_0,\,\varepsilon} \big\lVert \varepsilon - \varepsilon_\theta\big(\sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}\,x_0 + \sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}\,\varepsilon,\ t\big) \big\rVert^2 \)
Backup: DDPM

Training and sampling

Training

  1. \( x_0 \sim p_{\text{data}},\ \ t \sim \mathcal{U}\{1, \dots, T\},\ \ \varepsilon \sim \mathcal{N}(0, I) \)
  2. \( x_t = \sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}\,x_0 + \sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}\,\varepsilon \)
  3. gradient step on \( \big\lVert \varepsilon - \varepsilon_\theta(x_t, t) \big\rVert^2 \)
  4. repeat

Sampling

  1. \( x_T \sim \mathcal{N}(0, I) \)
  2. for \( t = T, \dots, 1 \): \( z \sim \mathcal{N}(0, I) \) if \( t > 1 \), else \( z = 0 \)
  3. \( \displaystyle x_{t-1} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\alpha_t}}\Big(x_t - \frac{\beta_t}{\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}}\,\varepsilon_\theta(x_t, t)\Big) + \sigma_t z \)
  4. \( \sigma_t^2 = \beta_t \) or \( \tilde\beta_t \) (both work)

One network call per step: \( T = 1000 \) calls per image.

Backup: DDPM ↔ score

Noise prediction is the score (Tweedie)

Best possible noise guess
\( \varepsilon^*(x_t, t) = \mathbb{E}[\varepsilon \mid x_t] = -\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}\ \nabla_{x_t} \log p_t(x_t) \)
Tweedie: best clean guess
\( \displaystyle \mathbb{E}[x_0 \mid x_t] = \frac{x_t + (1-\bar\alpha_t)\,\nabla_{x_t} \log p_t(x_t)}{\sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}} = \frac{x_t - \sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}\,\varepsilon^*}{\sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}} \)
Denoising score matching
\( \mathbb{E}\big\lVert s_\theta(x_t) - \nabla \log p_t(x_t) \big\rVert^2 = \mathbb{E}\big\lVert s_\theta(x_t) - \nabla_{x_t} \log q(x_t \mid x_0) \big\rVert^2 + C \)
Known per image
\( \displaystyle \nabla_{x_t} \log q(x_t \mid x_0) = -\frac{\varepsilon}{\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}} \)
Backup: Score SDE

From many small steps to an SDE

One DDPM step
\( x_t = \sqrt{1-\beta_t}\,x_{t-1} + \sqrt{\beta_t}\,\varepsilon_t, \qquad \varepsilon_t \sim \mathcal{N}(0, I) \)
Step size \( \Delta t = 1/T \)
\( \beta_t = \beta(t)\,\Delta t \)
Taylor
\( \sqrt{1-\beta(t)\,\Delta t} \approx 1 - \tfrac12\beta(t)\,\Delta t \)
So one step is
\( x_{t+\Delta t} - x_t \approx -\tfrac12\beta(t)\,x_t\,\Delta t + \sqrt{\beta(t)}\,\sqrt{\Delta t}\,\varepsilon \)
Brownian increment
\( \sqrt{\Delta t}\,\varepsilon \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \Delta t\, I) \ \hat=\ dW \)
Limit \( \Delta t \to 0 \)
\( dx = \underbrace{-\tfrac12\beta(t)\,x}_{f(x,\,t)}\,dt + \underbrace{\sqrt{\beta(t)}}_{g(t)}\,dW \)
Noise level
\( \bar\alpha_t = \textstyle\prod_s (1-\beta(s)\,\Delta t)\ \to\ \bar\alpha(t) = e^{-\int_0^t \beta(s)\,ds} \)
Backup: Score SDE

Running it back: Anderson 1982

Forward, \( t: 0 \to 1 \)
\( dx = f(x, t)\,dt + g(t)\,dW \)
Reverse, \( t: 1 \to 0 \)
\( dx = \big[f(x, t) - g(t)^2\,\nabla_x \log p_t(x)\big]\,dt + g(t)\,d\bar W \)
Guarantee
same marginals \( p_t \) at every \( t \): start at \( \mathcal{N}(0, I) \), end at \( p_{\text{data}} \)
For DDPM
\( dx = \big[-\tfrac12\beta(t)\,x - \beta(t)\,\nabla_x \log p_t(x)\big]\,dt + \sqrt{\beta(t)}\,d\bar W \)
Score from the network
\( {\displaystyle \nabla_x \log p_t(x) \approx s_\theta(x, t) = -\frac{\varepsilon_\theta(x, t)}{\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha(t)}}} \)
One sampling step
\( x_{t-\Delta t} = x_t + \big[\tfrac12\beta(t)\,x_t + \beta(t)\,s_\theta(x_t, t)\big]\,\Delta t + \sqrt{\beta(t)\,\Delta t}\ z, \qquad z \sim \mathcal{N}(0, I) \)
Backup: Score SDE

Why the ODE has the same \( p_t \)

Forward SDE
\( dx = f(x, t)\,dt + g(t)\,dW \)
Fokker–Planck
\( \partial_t p_t = -\nabla\cdot(f\,p_t) + \tfrac12 g^2\,\Delta p_t \)
Use \( \Delta p = \nabla\cdot(p\,\nabla \log p) \)
\( \partial_t p_t = -\nabla\cdot\Big[\big(f - \tfrac12 g^2\,\nabla \log p_t\big)\,p_t\Big] \)
Continuity equation of the ODE
\( dx = \big[f(x, t) - \tfrac12 g(t)^2\,\nabla_x \log p_t(x)\big]\,dt \)
VP SDE = DDPM
\( f = -\tfrac12 \beta(t)\,x, \quad g = \sqrt{\beta(t)}, \quad \bar\alpha(t) = e^{-\int_0^t \beta(s)\,ds} \)
Backup: DDIM

A forward process that skips steps

Keep every marginal
\( q_\sigma(x_t \mid x_0) = \mathcal{N}\big(\sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}\,x_0,\ (1-\bar\alpha_t) I\big) \ \Rightarrow\ \) same \( L_{\text{simple}} \), same network
New reverse step
\( \displaystyle q_\sigma(x_{t-1} \mid x_t, x_0) = \mathcal{N}\Big(\sqrt{\bar\alpha_{t-1}}\,x_0 + \sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_{t-1}-\sigma_t^2}\ \frac{x_t - \sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}\,x_0}{\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}},\ \sigma_t^2 I\Big) \)
Sampling step
\( x_{t-1} = \sqrt{\bar\alpha_{t-1}}\,\hat x_0 + \sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_{t-1}-\sigma_t^2}\ \varepsilon_\theta(x_t, t) + \sigma_t z, \quad \displaystyle \hat x_0 = \frac{x_t - \sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}\,\varepsilon_\theta}{\sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}} \)
How much noise
\( \displaystyle \sigma_t = \eta\,\sqrt{\frac{1-\bar\alpha_{t-1}}{1-\bar\alpha_t}}\sqrt{1-\frac{\bar\alpha_t}{\bar\alpha_{t-1}}}, \qquad \eta = 1:\ \text{DDPM}, \quad \eta = 0:\ \text{deterministic} \)
Skip steps
\( t \) runs over any subsequence \( \tau_1 < \dots < \tau_S \) of \( 1, \dots, T \): \( S \ll T \) network calls
Backup: DDIM

DDIM is Euler on the probability-flow ODE

Rescale (\( \eta = 0 \))
\( \displaystyle \bar x = \frac{x}{\sqrt{\bar\alpha}}, \qquad \sigma = \frac{\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha}}{\sqrt{\bar\alpha}} \)
One DDIM step
\( \displaystyle \frac{x_{t-1}}{\sqrt{\bar\alpha_{t-1}}} = \frac{x_t}{\sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}} + \Big(\sqrt{\frac{1-\bar\alpha_{t-1}}{\bar\alpha_{t-1}}} - \sqrt{\frac{1-\bar\alpha_t}{\bar\alpha_t}}\Big)\,\varepsilon_\theta(x_t, t) \)
= Euler step of
\( \displaystyle \frac{d\bar x}{d\sigma} = \varepsilon_\theta\Big(\frac{\bar x}{\sqrt{\sigma^2 + 1}},\ t(\sigma)\Big) \)
Which is
the probability-flow ODE of the VP SDE, in the coordinates \( (\bar x, \sigma) \)
Cost
Euler error per step \( \propto \Delta\sigma^2 \times \) curvature of the path: curved paths need many steps
Backup: Flow matching

A vector field that moves \( p_t \)

ODE
\( {\displaystyle \frac{dx}{dt}} = u_t(x) \)
\( u_t \) generates \( p_t \) iff
\( \partial_t p_t + \nabla\cdot(p_t\,u_t) = 0 \) (continuity equation)
Our path (\( t = 0 \) data, \( t = 1 \) noise)
\( x_t = (1-t)\,x_0 + t\,\varepsilon, \qquad x_0 \sim p_{\text{data}},\ \varepsilon \sim \mathcal{N}(0, I) \)
Velocity of one pair
\( {\displaystyle \frac{d x_t}{dt}} = \varepsilon - x_0 \)
Velocity of the whole cloud
\( u_t(x) = \mathbb{E}\big[\,\varepsilon - x_0 \ \big|\ x_t = x\,\big] \)
Backup: Flow matching

Why regressing \( \varepsilon - x_0 \) works

Want (intractable)
\( \mathcal{L}_{\text{FM}}(\theta) = \mathbb{E}_{t,\,x_t} \big\lVert v_\theta(x_t, t) - u_t(x_t) \big\rVert^2 \)
Train (tractable)
\( \mathcal{L}_{\text{CFM}}(\theta) = \mathbb{E}_{t,\,x_0,\,\varepsilon} \big\lVert v_\theta(x_t, t) - (\varepsilon - x_0) \big\rVert^2 \)
Cross terms agree
\( \mathbb{E}\big[v_\theta \cdot (\varepsilon - x_0)\big] = \mathbb{E}\big[v_\theta \cdot \mathbb{E}[\varepsilon - x_0 \mid x_t]\big] = \mathbb{E}\big[v_\theta \cdot u_t(x_t)\big] \)
So
\( \mathcal{L}_{\text{FM}} = \mathcal{L}_{\text{CFM}} + C \): same gradients, same minimizer \( v^* = u_t \)
Sampling (Euler, \( t: 1 \to 0 \))
\( x_{t-\Delta t} = x_t - \Delta t\ v_\theta(x_t, t) \)
Backup: Flow matching ↔ diffusion

Same information, different parameterization

From the velocity
\( \hat x_0 = x_t - t\,v_\theta(x_t, t), \qquad \hat\varepsilon = x_t + (1-t)\,v_\theta(x_t, t) \)
Score
\( {\displaystyle \nabla_x \log p_t(x_t) = -\frac{\mathbb{E}[\varepsilon \mid x_t]}{t} \approx -\frac{\hat\varepsilon}{t}} \)
General Gaussian path
\( x_t = a_t\,x_0 + b_t\,\varepsilon, \qquad u_t(x \mid x_0, \varepsilon) = \dot a_t\,x_0 + \dot b_t\,\varepsilon \)
Diffusion (VP)
\( a_t = \sqrt{\bar\alpha(t)}, \quad b_t = \sqrt{1-\bar\alpha(t)} \): curved per-pair paths
Rectified flow / linear
\( a_t = 1 - t, \quad b_t = t \): straight per-pair paths, constant speed
Backup: Rectified flow

Reflow: straighten the paths

1-rectified flow
train \( v^1 \) on independent pairs \( (x_0, \varepsilon) \)
New pairs
\( \varepsilon \sim \mathcal{N}(0, I), \quad \hat x_0 = \text{ODE solve of } v^1 \text{ from } \varepsilon \) (\( t: 1 \to 0 \))
Reflow
train \( v^2 \) on \( x_t = (1-t)\,\hat x_0 + t\,\varepsilon \) with target \( \varepsilon - \hat x_0 \)
Straightness
\( S(v) = \int_0^1 \mathbb{E}\big\lVert (\varepsilon - x_0) - v(x_t, t) \big\rVert^2 dt \): \( 0 \) means straight paths
Liu et al. 2023
marginals preserved, no convex transport cost increases, \( S \) decreases: one Euler step \( \hat x_0 = \varepsilon - v(\varepsilon, 1) \) becomes accurate
Backup: Sampling cost

Why fewer steps is hard

One step from pure noise
\( \hat x_0 = \mathbb{E}[x_0 \mid x_1] \approx \mathbb{E}[x_0] \): the average of all images, blurry
Euler error per step
\( {\displaystyle x(t-\Delta t) - \big(x_t - \Delta t\,v\big) = \tfrac12 \Delta t^2\,\frac{dv}{dt}\Big|_{\text{path}} + O(\Delta t^3)} \)
Curvature = error
straight path: \( \dfrac{dv}{dt} = 0 \Rightarrow \) exact in one step; curved path: errors add up over \( 1/\Delta t \) steps
Ways out
better solvers (Heun, DPM-Solver), straighter paths (reflow), distillation into 1–4 steps (next talks)
